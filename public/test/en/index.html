Benkowo Białowieża

A family place shaped over two decades. Not a hotel, not a guesthouse and not a collection of rooms for rent.

You arrive together. You stay without strangers. Two houses, a garden and time at your own pace.

Benkowo is designed for families, several families travelling together, groups of friends and small teams who want shared time without giving up personal space.

A smaller group may rent the entire property too. We never add other guests — privacy does not depend on the number of occupied beds.

Freedom does not mean anonymity. We welcome people who feel at home here and care for the place as they would for their own.

The story of Benkowo

20

years of family history

It was not built from scratch as a rental.

Benkowo became part of our family around 20 years ago. Our parents bought the homestead from a married couple of teachers and gradually adapted it for more comfortable living without erasing what was already here.

The work preserved the wooden character of the house, the scale of the buildings and Białowieża's local architectural context. New functions were meant to belong to the place, not replace its character.

The name Benkowo comes from Benedykt. It is the name of a family place, not a label invented for tourist accommodation.

One homestead, several rhythms

Together, without sharing every moment or every room.

A large group stays comfortably when everyone can meet around one table and later retreat to a quiet space of their own.
