Start

Loop

Allow 4–5 hours riding. Mixed surfaces materially affect pace.

Topiło

Carry water and food; do not assume food or toilets are open.

Return

Leave daylight margin. The sauna is part of the stay, not a route-operator service.

Plan B

In rain, mud, wind or doubtful fitness abandon the loop and choose a museum or short walk.

Limitations and map

This is not an easy family ride. We use a cautious 4–5 hours riding plus stops.
