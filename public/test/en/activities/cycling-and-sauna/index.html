A day idea from Benkowo

Cycling through the forest landscape

A few hours by bike, a break in Białowieża and a quiet evening in the sauna.

A bicycle lets you see more without turning the day into a sporting challenge. A well-chosen loop can be the main activity before everyone returns to Benkowo.

Who is it for?

Adults, older children and groups who enjoy an active day without racing.

A simple outline

  1. A route matched to the group
  2. A rest stop in Białowieża
  3. An evening in the wood-fired sauna

Check before you go

Check the bicycles, forecast, route length and where cycling is permitted.

Adapt the route to the weather, surface conditions and access rules in protected areas.
