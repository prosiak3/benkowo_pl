Orthodox church trail: Białowieża and the villages

A ready-made route joining Białowieża's ceramic iconostasis, Hajnówka's extraordinary cathedral and intimate village churches. Choose a short version or spend a full day among the colourful churches of the Land of Open Shutters.

Nearby version: four stops

1. Białowieża

St Nicholas Church with its unique ceramic iconostasis.

2. Hajnówka

Holy Trinity Cathedral, with bold modern architecture and a strong choral music tradition.

3. Dubiny

A large wooden Church of the Dormition in an old Podlasie village.

4. Nowoberezowo

Two contrasting churches close together: wooden St John the Theologian and the masonry Ascension Church.

Full-day extension

Continue from Nowoberezowo to Trześcianka, Soce and Puchły. The green church in Trześcianka, wooden homesteads in Soce and the blue church in Puchły make a natural finale.

Visit respectfully

These are active places of worship. Avoid services, dress appropriately and ask before photographing interiors. Churches are not always open outside worship times.
