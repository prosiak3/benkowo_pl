Barbara Bańka Atelier

An independent gallery and studio with original paintings, illustrations, Fine Art prints and authored souvenirs. Art classes can occasionally be arranged. It is close to Benkowo, but is not part of Benkowo.

Park Dyrekcyjny and nearby

Small local art shops

Small shops around Park Dyrekcyjny offer paintings, crafts and souvenirs. They are shops, not studios — ask for the maker's name and where the piece was made.

Paintings

Forest, bison and local architecture.

Crafts

Wood, prints, textiles and small locally made objects.

Souvenirs

The best ones are signed by a real maker rather than mass-produced.

An easy art walk

Arrange Barbara Bańka Atelier first, then walk through the shops around Park Dyrekcyjny.
