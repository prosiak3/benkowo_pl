Practical first

A realistic outline

Before booking

Call the operator. Confirm snow, price, minimum group, duration, meeting point, route, torches, menu and bonfire.

Arrival

Reach the agreed meeting point early and dress for prolonged cold.

The ride

Follow the driver’s instructions. Children remain under adult supervision.

Bonfire

Only at a place legally arranged by the operator; food and drink must be agreed in advance.

Plan B

If there is too little snow, ask whether a carriage ride is possible — only after explicit confirmation. Otherwise choose the museum and a quiet evening at Benkowo.

Limits and safety

We do not publish a price, duration, menu or meeting point without the operator’s confirmation. The bonfire is not an independent forest activity. This is an external service.
