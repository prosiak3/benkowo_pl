A distinct event in the local calendar

Forest Night Way of the Cross

About 23 kilometres of night pilgrimage from Białowieża through Forest villages to Hajnówka. It is a practice of faith and a demanding walk, not ordinary entertainment.

How the route works

Start in Białowieża

The latest verified edition began with Mass at St Teresa's church.

Forest villages

The route continued through Pogorzelce, Teremiski, Budy, Tryb Zwierzyniecki and Droga Hajnowska.

Finish in Hajnówka

Pilgrims reached the Poor Clares' monastery around midnight.

Check the new date

The latest verified edition took place on 27 March 2026. Check the organiser's announcement before planning a stay; a torch and reflective elements are among the essentials.
