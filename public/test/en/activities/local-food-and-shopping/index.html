Food cheat sheet

What to look for

Something sweet

Hajnówka Marcinek: thin cake layers with a cream filling and the region's most recognisable dessert.

Something hot

Potato babka or kiszka, kartacze dumplings or pelmeni, depending on the venue and day.

Something to take home

Honey, cheese, oils, grains, dried herbs, mushrooms and preserves from regional makers.

How to find them

The best products may appear at a seasonal market, in a small shop or directly from a producer. We do not invent a permanent market: choose a current stop on the day.
