Half-day plan

Everyone finds their pace

For swimmers

The sports pool has six 25-metre lanes.

For families

A leisure pool, two slides, lazy river, water jets and whirlpool.

For recovery

Dry and steam saunas plus a graduation tower help slow down after an active day.

Check the same-day notice

Standard opening hours are published by the venue, but competitions, holidays and maintenance can change them. Last admission is no later than 60 minutes before closing.
