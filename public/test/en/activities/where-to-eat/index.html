Białowieża

Stoczek 1929 for regional cooking, Carska for a special meal in the former station, or Dzika Malwa as another local option.

Hajnówka

Pair Babushka Bistro or Smaki Podlasia with the museum, cathedral or Water Park.

Along the way

Polana Żubra in Pogorzelce or Bojarski Gościniec in Narewka fit a day spent exploring by car.
