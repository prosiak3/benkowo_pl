Practical first

A realistic outline

The day before

Confirm the start time, meeting point, price, clothing and ground conditions with the guide.

Before dawn

Meet the SÓWECZKA guide and drive to the selected observation area.

Tracking

Look for tracks and scan the surroundings quietly; the walking section is usually 0–2 km.

Afterwards

Return for breakfast and rest. Even without a sighting, tracks and the guide’s forest knowledge make the trip worthwhile.

Plan B

A wild animal cannot be scheduled. If no bison appear, the guide focuses on tracks and species ecology. If conditions force cancellation, choose the Białowieża National Park museum.

Limits and safety

Stay at least 50 m from wild bison, do not leave the vehicle without instruction and follow the guide. A sighting is not guaranteed. This is operated by an independent provider.
