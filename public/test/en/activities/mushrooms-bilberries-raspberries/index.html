A season straight from the woods

Mushrooms, bilberries and raspberries

A basket, good shoes and a few quiet hours in the woods. Bilberries and raspberries in summer; mushrooms in autumn.

Summer · autumn · foraging
Time
2–4 hours
Season
Summer and autumn
For whom
Families and amateur foragers
Transport

Bring

Pace

More than a forest walk

Morning

Choose ordinary managed woodland outside no-picking areas.

Picking

Collect only fruit and mushroom species you can identify with certainty.

Return

Sort your finds at Benkowo and plan a shared meal.

The simple rule

Do not collect in reserves or closed areas. Leave any unknown mushrooms where they are.

Your host can suggest a simple seasonal direction without turning it into a complicated expedition.
