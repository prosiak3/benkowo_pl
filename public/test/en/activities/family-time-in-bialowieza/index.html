Details first

A realistic day sequence

Before leaving

Check current BNP notices and museum hours.

BNP Museum

The exhibition takes about 55 minutes. Last admission is one hour before closing.

Palace Park

Allow 45–90 minutes. Dogs must be on a lead and wear a muzzle.

Meal

Choose a venue after checking hours and space for the whole group.

Plan B

In severe weather keep the museum and meal; shorten or postpone the walk.

Limitations and map

Check live BNP refurbishment and access notices on the day. Benkowo does not guarantee a table or discount.
