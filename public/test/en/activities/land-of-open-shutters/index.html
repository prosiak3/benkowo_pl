Trześcianka

Decorated wooden homes and the distinctive green Church of St Michael the Archangel.

Soce

An intimate village of traditional homesteads, carved details and an unhurried rhythm.

Puchły

The blue Church of the Protection of the Mother of God, one of Podlasie's best-known wooden landmarks.

These are living villages

Homes and yards are private. Take photographs from public roads, do not enter property without an invitation, and keep quiet around active places of worship.
