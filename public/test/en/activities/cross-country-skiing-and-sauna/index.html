A day idea from Benkowo

Cross-country skiing and a warm sauna

A winter day shaped by movement, snow conditions and a warm return to Benkowo.

When conditions are right, cross-country skis offer a quiet way to experience the winter Forest. A sauna at Benkowo can complete the day.

Who is it for?

Active guests and beginners who choose a route suited to their ability.

A simple outline

  1. Hire suitable equipment
  2. Choose a route for the conditions
  3. Return and warm up in the sauna

Check before you go

Confirm snow conditions, equipment hire and the current state of the routes.

This is seasonal and depends on snow, equipment and route availability.
