A day idea from Benkowo

A full day at Benkowo

The garden, shared cooking, sauna and a day that does not depend on attraction opening times.

Not every day needs an outing. Benkowo makes it easy to divide time between meals together, the garden, rest and activities on the property.

Who is it for?

Every group, particularly after a busy day or when the weather is changeable.

A simple outline

  1. Breakfast without hotel hours
  2. Garden, playground and the glass Akwarium room
  3. Cooking together and an evening under the shelter

Check before you go

Agree on a shared meal time and sauna use; the rest of the day can remain open.

The whole plan uses spaces available directly on the property.
