# Benkowo Białowieża

> A private family property in Białowieża, rented in its entirety to one group. Two houses, a garden and a wood-fired sauna for up to 14 guests.

- Telephone: +48 602 761 082

- Maximum number of guests: 14

- Bedrooms: 6 in total
- Bathrooms: 4 in total
- Kitchens: 2
- Parking: 4 cars fit comfortably inside the property; an additional car may be left outside the gate

Benkowo does not rent individual rooms and never adds unrelated guests. A smaller group may rent the entire property if it accepts the minimum price for the whole property.

## Who Benkowo is for

Benkowo is intended primarily for one large family, two or three families travelling with children, multi-generational gatherings and trusted groups of friends. Quiet, small-team retreats may also be possible.

The property is not positioned for stag or hen parties, loud events, large corporate parties or stays driven solely by the lowest possible price.

## Main House

The Main House stands by Stoczek Street. It contains:

- 5 bedrooms;
- 3 bathrooms;
- a kitchen;
- a living room;
- a glass-walled veranda known as the “Aquarium”, facing the garden.

## Garden House

The second independent building stands deeper inside the property. It contains:

- 1 downstairs bedroom;
- an attic sleeping area with 4 beds;
- a separate kitchen;
- a bathroom;
- a garage for 1 car.

## Garden and shared outdoor space

The property is long and filled with mature trees. Granite paths lead from the entrance through the grounds. The property includes:

- a separate wood-fired sauna building;
- a covered outdoor shelter;
- a firepit and barbecue area;
- a small children's play area;
- a lawn;
- a grapevine pergola;
- fruit trees;
- large spruce trees by the Main House;
- a small spruce grove at the far end of the property.

## History

Benkowo has been in the family for around 20 years. The parents of the current hosts bought the homestead from a married couple of teachers. Over the following years, they adapted it for more comfortable use while respecting the existing buildings, the wooden character of the house and Białowieża's local architectural context.

The name Benkowo comes from the name Benedykt. It is the name of a family place rather than a label invented solely for tourist accommodation.

## Białowieża and ideas for a stay

Depending on the season and current availability, guests can consider walks and excursions in the Białowieża Forest, cycling, mushroom picking in areas where it is permitted, public tennis courts and mini-golf. Cross-country ski equipment may be available for hire in winter. The nearby town of Hajnówka has an indoor swimming pool and a seasonal ice rink.

Activities outside the property operate independently of Benkowo, may involve additional charges and always require current opening times, availability and rules to be checked.
